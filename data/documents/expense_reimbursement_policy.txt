MERIDIAN ANALYTICS LTD - TRAVEL AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Version 2.1, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance Department.

1. PRINCIPLES
Employees are expected to spend company money as carefully as they would their own. Only reasonable, necessary, and business-related expenses are reimbursed. This policy applies to all employees and contractors who incur expenses on behalf of Meridian.

2. SUBMITTING EXPENSES
Expenses are submitted through the finance portal under Expenses, then New Claim. Each claim must include an itemised receipt, the business purpose, and the project code. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted later than 60 days will be rejected. Approved claims are paid in the next monthly payroll run.

3. APPROVAL LIMITS
Claims up to 250 USD are approved by the employee's line manager. Claims from 250 USD to 2,500 USD require approval from the Head of Department. Claims above 2,500 USD require approval from the Finance Director. Employees cannot approve their own claims.

4. AIR TRAVEL
Flights under 4 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights over 4 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires written approval from a Director. Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance through the company travel portal. Last-minute bookings need a short explanation.

5. ACCOMMODATION
Hotels must be booked through the travel portal. The nightly limit is 180 USD in most cities and 260 USD in high-cost cities such as London, New York, San Francisco, Zurich, and Tokyo. Stays longer than 5 consecutive nights should be discussed with Finance because serviced apartments can be cheaper.

6. MEALS AND ENTERTAINMENT
When travelling, employees receive a daily meal allowance of 70 USD, covering breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursed except when entertaining clients, and then only up to 2 drinks per person. Client entertainment must not exceed 120 USD per person and requires a list of attendees.

7. GROUND TRANSPORT
Employees should use public transport where practical. Taxis and rideshares are reimbursed when public transport is unavailable, when carrying heavy equipment, or after 21:00. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at 0.62 USD per mile. Parking fees and tolls are reimbursed with a receipt. Car rental requires manager approval and must be a compact or mid-size vehicle.

8. NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS
The following are not reimbursed: traffic fines, personal entertainment, minibar items, hotel gym fees, spouse or family travel, and upgrades purchased for personal comfort.

9. CORPORATE CARDS
Employees who travel more than 6 times per year may request a corporate card. Card statements must be reconciled with receipts by the 5th of each month. Cards that are repeatedly unreconciled will be suspended.
