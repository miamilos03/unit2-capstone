MERIDIAN ANALYTICS LTD - LEAVE AND TIME OFF POLICY
Version 2.5, effective 1 March 2026. Owner: People and Culture Team.

1. SCOPE
This policy covers all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors and interns are covered by their individual agreements. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to 10 public holidays. Employees who have completed 5 or more years of service receive 2 additional days, and those with 10 or more years of service receive 4 additional days. New joiners receive leave pro-rata from their start date.

3. REQUESTING ANNUAL LEAVE
Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal under Time Off, then New Request. Requests of up to 3 consecutive days should be submitted at least 5 working days in advance. Requests longer than 3 days should be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance. Managers must respond within 3 working days. If the manager does not respond within that period, the request escalates to the Head of Department.

4. CARRY-OVER
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire without compensation. Any days above the carry-over limit are forfeited at the end of the year, unless the employee was prevented from taking leave due to a business-critical project approved in writing by a Director.

5. SICK LEAVE
Employees receive up to 10 paid sick days per year. Employees must notify their manager before their normal start time on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than 3 consecutive working days. Long-term illness is handled case by case with the People and Culture Team.

6. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of paid parental leave at full salary. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks at full salary. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption. Employees must give at least 8 weeks of notice where possible.

7. BEREAVEMENT AND COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
Employees receive 5 paid days following the death of an immediate family member, and 2 paid days for other close relatives. Additional unpaid leave can be agreed with the line manager.

8. UNPAID LEAVE AND SABBATICALS
Employees with at least 4 years of service may apply for a sabbatical of up to 3 months of unpaid leave. Applications must be submitted at least 6 months in advance and are approved based on business needs. The employee keeps their job, and benefits continue for the first 4 weeks.

9. OTHER PAID LEAVE
Employees receive 1 paid volunteering day per year and 2 paid days for study or examinations when the course is approved by their manager.
