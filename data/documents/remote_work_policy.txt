MERIDIAN ANALYTICS LTD - REMOTE AND HYBRID WORK POLICY
Version 1.8, effective 15 February 2026. Owner: People and Culture Team.

1. OVERVIEW
Meridian Analytics operates a hybrid model. We believe teams do their best work when they combine focused time at home with collaborative time together in the office. This policy sets out the expectations for all employees whose role allows remote work.

2. STANDARD HYBRID SCHEDULE
Employees are expected to work from one of our offices at least 3 days per week. Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days when all members of a team should be in the office. The remaining day or days can be chosen by agreement between the employee and their team lead.

3. FULLY REMOTE ROLES
Some roles are designated fully remote at the point of hiring. Fully remote employees must attend two in-person team gatherings per year, with travel costs paid by the company. A change from hybrid to fully remote requires written approval from the Head of Department and is reviewed after 6 months.

4. WORKING FROM ANOTHER LOCATION
Employees may work from a different city within their country of employment for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with manager approval. Working from another country is limited to 10 working days per year and requires prior approval from People and Culture, because of tax, immigration, and data protection rules. Employees must never work from another country without approval.

5. CORE HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Employees should be reachable during these hours and keep their calendar up to date. Outside core hours, employees may flex their schedule as long as they complete their contracted hours and meet deadlines. Employees are not expected to respond to messages outside their working hours except during declared incidents.

6. HOME OFFICE EQUIPMENT
Meridian provides a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard, and a mouse for remote work. Employees may also claim a one-time home office allowance of 400 USD for a chair, desk, or other ergonomic equipment. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase. A monthly internet stipend of 40 USD is paid to employees working remotely at least 2 days per week.

7. SECURITY WHEN WORKING REMOTELY
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office. Work devices must not be used by family members or friends. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the VPN switched on. Confidential calls should not be held in public places where they can be overheard.

8. HEALTH AND SAFETY
Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe working environment at home. People and Culture can arrange a free virtual ergonomic assessment on request. Any work-related injury at home must be reported within 48 hours.

9. REVIEW OF ARRANGEMENTS
Managers may change a remote arrangement with 4 weeks of notice if performance or business needs require it. Employees may appeal through the People and Culture Team.
